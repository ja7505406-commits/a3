Craftsmanship & Amber Gemology Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Amber Purse Fox, Manhattan Atelier

1. General Leathercraft Notice

The Baltic amber gemology treatises, succinic acid chemical profiles, leather saddle stitching guides, and couture handbag recommendations published across the Amber Purse Fox digital platforms and at our flagship salon located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, are presented exclusively for general educational, historical, and aesthetic appreciation. While our master leather artisans make every reasonable effort to ensure that all published technical guidance reflects verified leathercraft science, Amber Purse Fox makes no therapeutic or medical claims regarding succinic acid contact.

2. Amber Gemstone Maintenance & Thermal Sensitivity

Natural Baltic amber is an organic fossilized resin rated 2.0 to 2.5 on the Mohs hardness scale. While amber is remarkably resilient, extreme thermal heat, alcohol-based perfumes, harsh chemical solvents, and abrasive cleaning pads can scratch its lustrous surface or cause micro-fracturing. Patrons must follow our certified microfiber polishing and botanical balm care guidelines to preserve the deep golden transparency and refractive luster of their minaudières across generations of use.

3. Vegetable-Tanned Leather Patina & Aging Dynamics

Our full-grain Tuscan bridle leathers are tanned exclusively with natural tree bark extracts without synthetic plastic polyurethane coatings. Because vegetable-tanned leather absorbs ambient light and natural skin oils, it develops a deep, individual patina that darkens naturally over years of wear. These evolving surface tonal depths, subtle marbling nuances, and natural grain textures are authentic hallmarks of genuine vegetable leather rather than material flaws.

4. Bespoke Sizing & Hand-Carved Variations

Our digital photography and interactive configurator are calibrated to reflect the luminous golden amber, deep cognac, and rich chestnut tones of our handbags with high fidelity. However, because each minaudière panel is individually hand-carved from natural rough amber nodules and stitched by master tailors, subtle variances of several millimeters in bezel setting or leather grain are natural hallmarks of bespoke craft. Patrons are invited to inspect raw amber specimens at our Mercer Street salon prior to finalizing commissions.

5. Scientific References & Gemology Citations

Our editorial journal frequently cites international gemological archives, paleontology papers, and leathercraft history journals to provide scientific context for our patrons. These scholarly citations are curated solely to enhance your gemstone appreciation and leather understanding. Amber Purse Fox exercises no administrative authority, editorial control, or technical oversight over third-party gemological institutes or referenced academic laboratories, and we assume no legal liability for external research publications.
