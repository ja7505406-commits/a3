Privacy Policy & Patron Data Stewardship

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Amber Purse Fox, Manhattan Atelier

1. Introduction & Luxury Leathercraft Confidentiality

At Amber Purse Fox, operating our bespoke leathergoods atelier and amber gemology showroom at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, we hold client confidentiality and personal privacy to the highest standard of haute couture craftsmanship. We recognize that commissioning bespoke Baltic amber evening minaudières, scheduling private fitting sessions, or ordering custom bridal handbag suites involves sharing personal design preferences and confidential billing records. This Privacy Policy outlines the exact protocols we employ to collect, process, safeguard, and retain your data across our digital portals and Manhattan salon.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you engage with our leathergoods atelier, we collect essential client information including your full name, email address, telephone number, billing records, custom monogramming requests, and bespoke handbag dimensions recorded during private salon viewings. Additionally, when you utilize our interactive amber purse configurator or browse our gemology research treatises, our web servers record technical telemetry such as IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and referral paths via Google Tag manager. These technical metrics enable our digital artisans to optimize browsing performance and protect transaction security across international luxury fashion markets.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate artisan manufacturing and commercial operations, including fulfilling custom handbag commissions, coordinating fitting salon appointments, organizing personalized leather monogramming, generating formal invoices, and delivering insured shipment tracking updates. Furthermore, with your explicit consent, we may send private invitations to closed-door Baltic amber specimen previews and seasonal leather collection unveils. We maintain an absolute policy never to sell, rent, monetize, or publicly distribute your personal information or handbag specifications to any third-party marketing brokers.

4. Data Storage & Cryptographic Protection Standards

All digital commission archives, client handbag patterns, and billing information entrusted to Amber Purse Fox are stored on private cloud servers shielded by 256-bit AES encryption protocols and strict administrative access controls. Digital payment transactions are handled directly through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant processors, ensuring our atelier never retains raw credit card numbers on local hardware. We perform continuous vulnerability assessments and cybersecurity audits to guarantee that your private information remains completely protected from unauthorized external access.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Access Controls

Regardless of your country of residence, Amber Purse Fox extends comprehensive data privacy rights aligned with global standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You retain the absolute legal right to inspect the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to profile records, restrict processing activities, or demand the permanent deletion of your client records from our systems. To exercise these statutory privacy rights, simply submit a formal request to our concierge desk at any time.

6. Data Retention Protocols & Archival Integrity

We retain client bespoke patterns and gemstone registries for as long as your patron profile remains active, enabling seamless repeat orders, seasonal leather expansions, and lifetime handbag restoration services without requiring repetitive intake sessions. If you choose to conclude your patronage or request data expungement, all electronic records and custom handbag patterns are securely purged and permanently deleted within thirty business days, subject only to mandatory statutory tax and accounting retention requirements mandated by United States commercial law.

7. Atelier Contact for Privacy Governance

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to discuss our client confidentiality practices with our leadership team, please contact our administrative desk. Written correspondence should be addressed directly to Amber Purse Fox, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically to our concierge staff by calling +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours. Our compliance team will examine your communication with professional care and respond within standard business timelines.
